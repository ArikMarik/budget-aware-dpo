## 7. CRITICAL ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### Issue 1: ONE problem has 5.2 MILLION pairs 🔴🔴🔴
- This single problem accounts for 43% of all pairs!
- Model will massively overfit to this problem

### Issue 2: Complex Imbalance (82% Hard vs 18% Easy) 🔴
- The model will not learn the "Easy = short preferred" behavior properly

### Issue 3: Almost NO correctness rejections (0.2% vs 99.8%) 🔴
- The model will barely learn that "wrong answer = rejected"
- Almost all rejections are "too long" or "too short" - length only!

### Issue 4: 67% of problems have 100+ pairs 🔴
- Massive overfitting risk across many problems

---

## 8. Recommendations for Quality-Focused Subset

### Must Fix Issues

1. **Cap at 4 pairs per problem** - immediate!
   - Reduces to ~5,840 pairs from 12.2M
   
2. **Balance complexity** - stratify to 50/50 Easy/Hard
   - Current: 18% Easy, 82% Hard
   - Target: 50% Easy, 50% Hard
   
3. **Add more correctness rejections** - target at least 30%
   - Current: 0.2% correctness rejections
   - Target: 30% correctness, 70% length

### Subsampling Strategy

```
Step 1: Cap at 4 pairs per problem
Step 2: Stratify by complexity (50/50)
Step 3: Ensure 30% correctness rejections
Step 4: Remove extreme cases (>100 pairs)
```

## 9. Root Cause Analysis

The preprocessing is creating way too many pairs because:
1. It creates Cartesian products: all preferred × all rejected
2. Many problems have 1000s of solutions → millions of pairs
3. The "incorrect" label is underflowing (almost no incorrect solutions)

This needs fixing in `preprocessing.py` before training!
